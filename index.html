About Me

Welcome to my corner of the web! Writing has always been a source of genuine joy for me, and this blog serves as a home for my thoughts, lessons, and continuous learning journey.

With over 20 years of experience in custody banking software, my work is driven by a passion for identifying complex problems and crafting creative solutions. I firmly believe in acting as a true business partner rather than just a technology order taker, ensuring that strategy and technical execution walk hand in hand. Beyond building software, I have a deep love for teaching and sharing knowledge, using both my writing and mentorship to make complex ideas accessible and engaging.

Outside of my professional life, I remain a dedicated practitioner of yoga and chanting—practices that ground me, cultivate mindfulness, and keep me centered in everything I do. Thank you for stopping by, and I hope you find value and inspiration in these pages!
