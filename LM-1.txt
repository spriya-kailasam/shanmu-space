Legacy system modernization is the talk of the town. Before modernizing the data warehouse, ask yourself: “𝘋𝘰 𝘺𝘰𝘶 𝘳𝘦𝘢𝘭𝘭𝘺 𝘶𝘯𝘥𝘦𝘳𝘴𝘵𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘺𝘰𝘶𝘳 𝘥𝘢𝘵𝘢?”

Over the years, legacy systems have accumulated rich business logic that drives critical operations. However, they also carry redundant, unused, and outdated logic.

This logic is rarely in one place - It is spread across multiple layers running on different tech stacks. Connecting these scattered pieces to see the full picture is often “the hardest part”.

Modernization is not about copying what exists into a new platform. It is about understanding what truly matters today and rebuilding with clarity. Without this understanding, modernization risks carrying forward inefficiencies into modern systems - this tends to pollute the system and creates long-term challenges in scaling, productivity, and growth.

Data lineage plays a key role here - it helps decode existing logic, trace data flow, and separate what is useful from what is not. It gives visibility into how data is created, transformed, and consumed across system - something that is often missing in legacy environments.

Without clear lineage, teams spend weeks trying to understand a single report or data flow. With lineage, this understanding becomes structured, faster, and more reliable.

That is why data lineage is not just a support activity - it is the first step in any meaningful data warehouse modernization.

Only when you truly understand your data you can modernize with confidence.
